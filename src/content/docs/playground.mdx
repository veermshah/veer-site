The real Veer compiler, planner, and simulated engine, compiled to WebAssembly and running in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server. Pick an example or write your own, then:

- **Check**: type-check and verify budgets (`veer check`)
- **Plan**: proven peak KV memory, prefill and decode tokens, sharing, splice costs, context DAG (`veer plan`)
- **Types**: inferred signatures, including tensor error bounds (`veer types`)
- **Run**: execute `main` on the simulated engine and compare measured numbers with the plan (`veer run --stats`)

Try breaking things: delete a `drop`, use a context twice, lower a `@budget`, or add `@accuracy(rel <= 0.25)` to `mlp_mxfp4` in `quant_mlp.veer`.
